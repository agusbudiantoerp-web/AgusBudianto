📐 Rumus Lengkap — IGKM v3.0
ℹ️ Semua rumus menggunakan satuan MENIT secara konsisten. CT per swing diinput dalam DETIK dan dikonversi otomatis.
1 · CT Excavator (Waktu Loading per DT)
STEP 1 — Hitung Jumlah Bucket (Pass Count) Volume LCM per bucket = Bucket_m3 × Fill_factor Pass Count = CEILING( Vessel_DT_m3 / Volume_LCM_per_bucket ) STEP 2 — Hitung Total Waktu Loading (CT_Loading) CT_swing_mnt = CT_swing_detik / 60 CT_Loading_mnt = Pass_Count × CT_swing_mnt Contoh: Bucket=2.2m³, FF=87%, Vessel=18m³, CT_swing=28dtk LCM/bucket = 2.2 × 0.87 = 1.914 m³ Pass Count = CEILING(18 / 1.914) = CEILING(9.41) = 10 pass CT_Loading = 10 × (28/60) = 10 × 0.467 = 4.67 menit
2 · CT Dump Truck — Mode Otomatis
CT_DT = CT_Loading + T_haul_isi + T_spot_dump + T_haul_kosong + T_antri CT_Loading = Pass_Count × CT_swing_mnt [menit] T_haul_isi = (Jarak_m / 1000) / V_loaded_eff × 60 [menit] T_haul_kosong = (Jarak_m / 1000) / V_empty_eff × 60 [menit] V_loaded_eff = V_loaded_km/h × Grade_factor × Road_cond_factor V_empty_eff = V_empty_km/h × Road_cond_factor ← grade membantu kembali
3 · Match Factor
MF = (N_DT × CT_Loading) / (N_Exca × CT_DT) N_DT = Jumlah Dump Truck CT_Loading = Waktu loading 1 DT oleh 1 Exca (menit) ← bukan CT_swing! N_Exca = Jumlah Excavator CT_DT = Total cycle time DT (menit) Contoh (data user): CT_Loading=5.2mnt, CT_DT=10mnt, N_DT=2, N_Exca=1 MF = (2 × 5.2) / (1 × 10) = 10.4 / 10 = 1.04 ✅
4 · Produktivitas Fleet per Jam ★ KUNCI
Produktivitas = trips/jam × BCM_per_trip × N_DT × Efisiensi trips_per_jam = 60 / CT_DT ← siklus per jam per unit DT BCM_per_trip = (Pass_Count × Bucket_m3 × FF) / (1 + Swell) Prod_BCM_jam = trips_per_jam × BCM_per_trip × N_DT × Eff_DT Prod_ton_jam = Prod_BCM_jam × Density_BCM Contoh verifikasi (BCM=10/trip, CT_DT=10mnt, N_DT=2, Eff=83%): trips/jam = 60 / 10 = 6 trips/jam per DT BCM/jam/DT = 6 × 10 = 60 BCM/jam Fleet BCM/jam = 60 × 2 DT × 0.83 = 99.6 BCM/jam ✅ Tanpa efisiensi (Eff=100%): 6 × 10 × 2 = 120 BCM/jam ✅
5 · Interpretasi MF
MF < 0.85
Under-trucked. DT kurang, Exca idle menunggu DT.
0.85–0.95
Mendekati optimal. Exca sedikit idle. Masih produktif.
0.95–1.10
OPTIMAL. Fleet balance. Target operasi tambang.
MF > 1.10
Over-trucked. DT mengantri di loading point.
